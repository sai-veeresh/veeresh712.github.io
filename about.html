About me

I am Sai Veeresh Bandi, a mechanical engineer from India with a research focus on computational astrophysics.

I work as a Product Design Engineer at HRBL Group in Gurugram, designing EV components and running thermal CFD. Outside work I build and run simulations of astrophysical flows, hypersonic and rarefied gases, and MHD, and I develop OpenFOAM solvers in C++.

I have also done freelance aerospace work and published an aerothermal CFD paper. I am open to relocating for a PhD or research role.

Education

B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering, University College of Engineering Narasaraopeta (JNTUK), 2016 to 2020.
