Astrophysics

Computational work on how stars evolve and how astrophysical gas flows.

Stellar evolution

Low-metallicity (Z = 10-4) stellar models computed with MESA for a grid of 0.5 to 2 solar masses, used to study zero-age main sequence properties and mass-luminosity scaling relations.

Astrophysical fluid dynamics

Hydrodynamics and MHD simulations with Athena++, PLUTO and RAMSES, including one-dimensional and two-dimensional Bondi accretion with and without a wind, and a review of relativistic fluid methods.

  • Bondi accretion: 1D, 2D, 2D with wind
  • Riemann solvers for relativistic flows
  • Long-term goal: astrophysical solvers in OpenFOAM
